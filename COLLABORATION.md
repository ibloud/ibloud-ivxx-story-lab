# Collaboration Request

## What is being requested

*The Signal Between Us* is ready for an initial, nonbinding evaluation. Dominique Devereaux / IBLoud IVXX Story Lab is seeking conversations with people who can help determine whether the concept should move into scripted development and, if so, on what ethical and contractual terms.

A response may address one or more of these lanes:

| Lane | Useful response |
|---|---|
| Creative evaluation | Notes on premise, character agency, structure, tone or format |
| Producer development | Interest in packaging, budgeting, scheduling or identifying the next deliverable |
| Screenwriting | Interest in developing the treatment and sample into a screenplay or pilot |
| Artist participation | A conversation about whether an artist or authorized representative wishes to evaluate a specifically bounded version |
| Entertainment law | Chain-of-title, name/likeness, music, fair-use, AI and collaboration review |
| Music | Original scoring, songwriting, supervision, replacement cues or clearance strategy |
| Research/institutional | Evaluation of provenance, platform memory, accessible archives or creator-compensation methods |

No recipient is being asked to validate speculation about a real person's private life.

## The immediate decision

The first decision is:

> Is there enough creative and methodological value here to justify a development conversation independent of any named artist's participation?

If yes, the next step is to define the proposed role, permitted materials, confidentiality expectations, ownership of new work, compensation expectations and decision authority before substantive collaboration begins.

## Response path

Use a new GitHub issue:

**[Open a project-evaluation inquiry](https://github.com/ibloud/ibloud-ivxx-story-lab/issues/new?title=Project%20evaluation%20inquiry&body=Role%20or%20organization%3A%0A%0AArea%20of%20interest%3A%0A%0AWhat%20you%20would%20like%20to%20evaluate%20or%20discuss%3A%0A%0APreferred%20next%20step%3A)**

Include:

- your role or organization;
- the lane you are responding to;
- what you want to evaluate or discuss; and
- the next step you propose.

Do **not** place private contact details, contracts, unreleased music, credentials, private communications or confidential business information in a public issue. After the identity and purpose of an inquiry are verified, the parties may choose a private channel.

Opening an issue does not create confidentiality, representation, employment, partnership, permission, option, license or an obligation to respond.

## Submission boundaries

Please do not submit:

- claims about a living person's private relationships, intentions or conduct;
- attempts to contact or pressure a story subject through this project;
- copyrighted recordings, lyrics, footage, photography, artwork or scripts without authority;
- synthetic replicas or imitations of a living person's voice or likeness;
- materials subject to another party's NDA or confidentiality duty; or
- proposals that assume artist, label, tour, legal or platform endorsement.

## Before any development work begins

The parties should record, at minimum:

- who is participating and in what capacity;
- whether the conversation is confidential;
- who owns pre-existing materials;
- how new material may be used;
- credit and compensation;
- whether AI tools or training uses are permitted;
- name, likeness, voice and performance permissions;
- approval, correction and takedown procedures;
- term, territory, media and exclusivity; and
- how either party may stop or leave the process.

See [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md), [WATERFALL.md](WATERFALL.md) and [CLEARANCE-LEDGER.md](CLEARANCE-LEDGER.md). Those files are development frameworks, not signed agreements or legal advice.
