I. Character Brief & Narrative Context

The character IBLoud IVXX serves as an archetype navigating platform saturation, identity construction, and musical witness[cite: 2]. Rather than delivering a linear story, her path is logged across decentralized nodes (Audio.com, Bluesky, Tumblr, and GitHub)[cite: 2].

Core Narrative Pillars

  • Platform Memory: Investigating how algorithms retain, warp, or forget creative artifacts over time[cite: 2].
  • Shadow Identity: The contrast between public spectacle and internal isolation (mirrored by the Everything's Strange Here era)[cite: 2].
  • Musical Witness: Auditable records of creative response without asserting false real-world claims[cite: 2].

II. Statutory Fair Use Rationale (17 U.S.C. § 107)

All work contained within this repository adheres strictly to the four-factor fair use analysis:

  1. Purpose and Character of Use: The project is transformative, educational, and noncommercial. It recontextualizes short musical and textual excerpts into an interactive narrative framework.
  2. Nature of the Copyrighted Work: Excerpts are drawn from publicly released music and promotional materials, analyzed strictly in a commentary context.
  3. Amount and Substantiality: Only brief fragments, metadata references, or public commentary are utilized—no full master recordings are hosted or redistributed.
  4. Effect Upon the Potential Market: This research prototype does not compete with or substitute for commercial releases. Links lead directly to official ticketing platforms and stream providers[cite: 2].
