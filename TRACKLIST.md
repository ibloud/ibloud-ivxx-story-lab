## Original soundtrack target

Create a six-cue original suite:

1. **Signal Without Sender** — sparse call tone, breath and broken percussion.
2. **Gerryland After Dark** — noir movement through a public persona-space.
3. **The Feed Decides** — escalating rhythmic duplication and interruption.
4. **Violet Witness** — IBLoud's character theme, written from her own perspective.
5. **No Private Knowledge** — the emotional pivot from projection to boundary.
6. **Endless, Mended** — an open ending oriented toward contribution.

Human-written lyrics should establish the story before generative arrangement. No prompt should request imitation of G-Eazy, Megan Thee Stallion, Logic, or another living artist.
