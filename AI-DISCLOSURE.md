# AI Assistance and Human Authorship Record

## Public disclosure

Some development recordings may use Suno or other generative tools for arrangement, instrumentation, synthetic performance or alternate treatments. AI assistance does not imply that a living artist participated in, endorsed or was intentionally replicated by the work.

## Missing-lyrics workflow

1. Write the character objective and scene conflict without referencing a celebrity's private thoughts.
2. Draft the chorus, key images and at least one complete verse through human authorship.
3. Record the dated human draft in `lyrics/human-drafts/`.
4. Use generative tools for bounded alternatives or musical demonstrations.
5. Save chosen output notes in `lyrics/ai-assisted-drafts/` without copying protected lyrics.
6. Revise substantively and record the human decisions that determine the final expression.
7. Complete the clearance ledger before public or commercial release.
