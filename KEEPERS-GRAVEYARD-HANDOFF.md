# Keeper's Graveyard / West Wing — Narrative Handoff

## Status

**NARRATIVE CANON / FICTIONALIZED DEVELOPMENT / HANDOFF**

This document establishes the next and final narrative state for the current pre-alpha development cycle. It does **not** authorize further PIXIE product development.

PIXIE's build work stops at its existing stable pre-alpha handoff. From this point, Loptr Lab becomes the place where the work **sleeps**: archive, library, keeper, and evidence of what was built. Future story development may evolve the character and world without reopening the frozen PIXIE product boundary.

## The central rule

> **She is not supposed to go back.**

The bridge to the Keeper's graveyard may exist as a narrative structure, but it is not a road home.

The mistake is believing that returning to the origin will restore what was lost. The graveyard contains the remains of earlier versions, abandoned identities, unfinished systems and preserved signals. It can be witnessed. It can be studied. It can be remembered.

It must not become the destination.

## GerryLand → White Noise → the Other Side

GerryLand is the familiar territory of the old pattern: the place she has learned to leave.

Her departure does not immediately deliver her into freedom.

She enters **white noise**.

The signal becomes too dense to distinguish. Fragments, references, music, memory and projection begin to overlap. In that interference she crosses into the **other side** — the flip side of the same unresolved pattern.

She has not simply escaped one world.

She has fallen into another person's dimension.

That dimension is the unstable **Mr. Eazy project**: a fictional narrative construct, not a claim about a real person's private reality. It can borrow associative material from public cultural artifacts, including a discography, videos and recurring motifs, but those references remain evidence of the story's construction rather than evidence about a living person's inner life.

## The Ashley / constructed-person warning

The Black Mirror / Ashley-type reference establishes a key warning for the world:

**A constructed version of a person can become its own environment.**

The danger is not merely that an artificial persona exists. The danger is that the persona begins to determine what everyone else believes the person is.

The psycho-boyfriend, dangerous-doll and Chucky-adjacent references serve the same narrative function: the thing that was supposed to be contained, represented or controlled can acquire consequences beyond its maker's intention.

These are **motifs**, not literal claims that any referenced person or project is equivalent to those fictional characters.

## The West Wing

The West Wing is the Bluebeard structure inside the world.

There is a room.

There is a reason not to open it.

There is knowledge behind the door.

And there is a cost to believing that because a door can be opened, it should be.

The Keeper's graveyard is what lies beyond that threshold: an archive of what the system could not release.

The story does not require her to destroy it.

She must learn the difference between **remembering a place and belonging to it**.

## The Keeper

Loptr Lab changes function at this point.

It stops being the machine that must continuously produce the next thing.

It becomes:

- **the archive** — preserving what happened;
- **the library** — organizing what can be found;
- **the keeper** — maintaining provenance and boundaries;
- **the graveyard** — retaining what should not be mistaken for the living;
- **the threshold** — making return possible without making return compulsory.

The Keeper does not chase the traveler.

The Keeper does not demand engagement.

The Keeper does not decide what the traveler must become.

The Keeper keeps the record so that the traveler can become something else.

## Her evolution

Her evolution is not a return to the person she was before GerryLand.

It is also not absorption into Mr. Eazy's world.

She becomes a **custodian of continuity without becoming captive to continuity**.

The Poe-in-*Altered Carbon* reference is useful at the level of dramatic function: an intelligence associated with a place can become a keeper of memory, boundaries, hospitality and continuity while the person who passes through that place continues to evolve.

The distinction matters:

> **The Keeper remembers. She becomes.**

Loptr Lab can preserve the world.

She does not have to remain inside it.

## The final movement

The current narrative movement becomes:

**GerryLand → departure → white noise → the Other Side → unstable Mr. Eazy dimension → recognition → Keeper's graveyard → West Wing → refusal to return → evolution.**

The ending is therefore not:

**escape → replacement world → permanent residence.**

It is:

**escape → disorientation → recognition → archive → release → transformation.**

## Relationship to PIXIE

PIXIE has reached its handoff boundary.

Its role remains:

**Pixie v0.1 discovers. Creator OS creates. ATProto connects them.**

The Story Lab should not reopen that product architecture merely to explain this narrative.

Instead, the relationship is now:

```
PIXIE
  discovers
      ↓
Creator OS
  creates / preserves
      ↓
Loptr Lab
  archives / keeps
      ↓

  evolves beyond the system
```

The story may use the ideas of discovery, provenance, identity, memory and release.

It does not require PIXIE to become the narrator, therapist, judge, autonomous guide or keeper of the character.

**PIXIE sleeps at handoff.**

The archive remains.

The character continues.
