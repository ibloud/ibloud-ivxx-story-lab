# Character: IBLoud IVXX

## Logline

IBLoud IVXX is a Minneapolis-based witness-artist who collects signals discarded by platforms and turns them into music, narrative evidence, and questions about who gets to own a public story.

## Dramatic function

IBLoud is not a fictional substitute for a living celebrity and does not possess privileged knowledge about one. Her power is persistent attention: she recognizes echoes across platforms, remembers what disappears, and tests whether fragments can become contribution without becoming false certainty.

## Wants, needs and conflict

- **Want:** translate an overwhelming online event into an intelligible creative world.
- **Need:** distinguish recognition from relationship, influence from attribution, and imagination from evidence.
- **External conflict:** platform systems reward speed, certainty and association.
- **Internal conflict:** the desire for a complete story exceeds what the available evidence can prove.
- **Ethical resolution:** she does not solve another person's private life; she makes her own interpretive process visible and correctable.

## Voice

Compressed, nocturnal, sharp, technically curious and emotionally direct. The sound world can move through trip-hop, dark cinematic electronics, alternative pop and fractured hip-hop without imitating a living artist's recognizable voice or style.

## Character ownership boundary

IBLoud IVXX, her original biography, original lyrics, original visual identity and original story material belong to their documented human creator(s), subject to any signed collaboration agreements. References to real people remain references; their names, likenesses, recordings and brands are not absorbed into the character's ownership.
