# 90-Second CTV Series Proof-of-Concept Trailer

## Logline

A public signal becomes rumor, music, character and archive—until one independent creator builds a laboratory to separate what happened, what was said, what was imagined and what can ethically be made from it now.

## Format

- Runtime: 90 seconds
- Master: 16:9, 3840×2160 or 1920×1080, 23.976 fps
- Companion crops: 9:16 and 1:1 after picture lock
- Tone: archival noir moving toward transparent research practice
- Score: original or properly licensed instrumental only
- Third-party material: placeholders until admitted under `FAIR-USE-PROOF-OF-CONCEPT.md`

## Five-part CTV promise

1. **The Signal** — establishes the public artifact and the limits of what it proves.
2. **Gerryland** — examines platform amplification and celebrity meaning-making.
3. **The Witness** — centers IBLoud IVXX’s documented creative response.
4. **The Laboratory** — demonstrates sourcing, truth labels, music replacement and rights infrastructure.
5. **The Return** — tests ethical discovery around the 2026 Endless Summer Tour without implying affiliation.

## Audio plan

- Begin with isolated modem/signal texture created or licensed for the prototype.
- Introduce an original 78–86 BPM pulse under **Gerryland**.
- Remove percussion under the four truth labels so the method is intelligible.
- Build with an original bass pulse toward **The Return**.
- End dry, allowing the non-affiliation statement to register.
- Do not use G-Eazy masters, compositions, mashups or sound-alike prompts as score.

## Success test

The trailer succeeds if a viewer can state: (1) this is an independent project, (2) the series studies how online meaning is made, (3) IBLoud IVXX is the witness and creative subject, (4) the archive distinguishes evidence from fiction, and (5) the 2026 tour is context—not proof of affiliation.
