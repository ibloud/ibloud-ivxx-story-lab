# Story Arc and Episode Engine

## Season structure

### Act I — Signal

A brief public artifact becomes disproportionately meaningful. The observer does not yet understand whether the signal is personal, cultural, accidental or algorithmic.

### Act II — Amplification

Platforms duplicate the fragment. Commentary hardens possibility into apparent fact. Music functions as navigation: every track opens another emotional interpretation rather than proving an external claim.

### Act III — Contradiction

Public statements and reliable reporting disrupt the spectator-created story. The archive must decide whether it exists to preserve certainty or preserve the record of uncertainty.

### Act IV — Laboratory

IBLoud becomes an active maker. She labels sources, replaces uncleared material, writes missing lyrics, documents AI assistance and builds an economic model that credits contributors.

### Act V — Return

A new Endless Summer tour makes the archive newly relevant. The question changes from “what happened?” to “what can this practice contribute now?”

### Act VI — Mending

The project publishes its method, boundaries and correction path. Audience attention is directed toward documented work and official ticket sources without suggesting affiliation.

## Repeatable episode pattern

Each episode or post should contain:

1. one dated artifact;
2. one truth-status label;
3. one character consequence;
4. one musical cue;
5. one research question;
6. one rights note; and
7. one contribution or correction path.

## Editorial rule

Association is allowed as form. Assertion requires evidence.
