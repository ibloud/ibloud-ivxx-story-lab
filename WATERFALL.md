# Provisional Rights and Revenue Waterfall

> **Development model only. Not an offer, promise, accounting statement or legal advice.** No participant owns a percentage or receives payment under this document. Adopt a waterfall only through signed agreements reviewed by qualified entertainment counsel, tax advisers and production accounting.

## What this model covers

This document concerns receipts earned by an independently financed adaptation of *The Signal Between Us* or related IBLoud IVXX production. It does **not** claim or redirect:

- G-Eazy, Logic or any other artist's catalogue income;
- songwriter/publisher or label/master royalties;
- Endless Summer Tour ticket, merchandise, sponsorship or promoter revenue;
- platform revenue belonging to Tumblr, Audio.com, YouTube or other services; or
- revenue from third-party footage, photographs, posts, trademarks or performances.

A license fee paid for a song, master, clip or likeness is a production cost or contracted participation—not a transfer of that owner's unrelated revenue stream.

## Receipts waterfall: attorney-review draft

“Gross Receipts” and every permitted deduction must be precisely defined in the governing agreements. The following is a sequence, not a forecast:

| Tier | Paid from project receipts | Required controls |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Taxes, refunds, chargebacks and sums collected for third parties | Defined exclusions; no double deduction |
| 1 | Collection-account charges, distributor fees and approved, documented distribution/delivery expenses | Expense caps; audit rights; no undisclosed affiliates |
| 2 | Approved sales-agent commission and capped, documented expenses | Written territory and channel scope |
| 3 | Senior production debt: principal and contracted interest | No payment beyond executed financing terms |
| 4 | Investor recoupment and any negotiated premium | Pari passu or priority position stated expressly |
| 5 | Approved deferred compensation | Named payees, amounts, triggers and accounting dates |
| 6 | Remaining Net Proceeds split between investor and producer pools | Exact definition negotiated; do not rely on “net profits” alone |
| 7 | Participant backend paid from the pool identified in each signed agreement | State the base, corridor, cap, term, statements and audit rights |
| 8 | Producer-pool mission allocation, if adopted | Applies only after Tier 6 and only by executed agreement |

No fee, percentage, premium or investor/producer split is fixed here.

## Experimental mission allocation

For research and negotiation, the project may model this allocation **inside the producer-side participation pool after all superior tiers are satisfied**:

| Producer-pool category | Illustrative share |
|---|---:|
| Human source contributors | 35% |
| Creative stewardship / project IP | 25% |
| Community creator or licensee participation | 20% |
| Public-benefit fund | 10% |
| Open infrastructure | 5% |
| Claims and correction reserve | 5% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

This is a values model, not adopted policy. Before use, agreements must define eligibility, ownership versus compensation, decision authority, recoupability, unclaimed funds, taxes, reporting, audits, termination and treatment of disputes.

## Music is a parallel rights system

For every song synchronized to picture:

1. identify and clear the composition through its owner/controller;
2. identify and clear the specific sound recording through its master owner/controller;
3. align both grants on media, territory, term, promotional/trailer use and other restrictions;
4. document fees, royalties, most-favored-nations terms and any backend separately; and
5. retain executed agreements in the chain-of-title file.

The usable scope is no broader than the intersection of the composition and master grants. A song cleared for the film may still be uncleared for a trailer, advertisement, soundtrack album or social clip.

## Chain-of-title packet before financing or release

- entity and operating agreement;
- screenplay/source-material assignment or license;
- dated authorship and AI-assistance records;
- producer, writer, director, cast, crew and composer agreements;
- investor and loan agreements;
- music sync and master-use licenses;
- clip, artwork, trademark, name/likeness, location and appearance releases;
- platform/tool terms captured for the dates used;
- distribution and sales-agent agreements;
- E&O review materials;
- clearance ledger, cue sheet and correction record; and
- collection-account instructions, statements and audit procedure.
