# Contributing Evidence or Corrections

## Mission and participation terms

Current open review and contribution opportunities are voluntary and unpaid. Before work begins, agree in writing on scope, time, what will be public, credit preferences, and an exit path. You can stop at any point. Participation does not promise employment, ownership, revenue share, academic credit, or future pay. Any paid commission or other formal arrangement requires a separate signed agreement before work begins. External assistance or benefits belong to the participant and are not compensation from Loptr Lab.

[People over profit, accessibility first, and our funding boundary](https://github.com/ibloud/ibloud.github.io/blob/main/MISSION.md).

Contributions are welcome when they improve accuracy, provenance, accessibility or rights documentation.

## Required for factual additions

- a stable source or creator-controlled archive;
- the date of the underlying event and the publication date;
- one truth-status label: `DOCUMENTED`, `ATTRIBUTED`, `INTERPRETIVE` or `FICTIONALIZED`;
- a brief explanation of what the source establishes; and
- confirmation that private personal information has been removed.

## Not accepted

- claims about private relationships or intentions based only on speculation;
- impersonation or synthetic replicas of living people;
- copied lyrics, stems, recordings, photographs or artwork without permission;
- language implying official tour or artist affiliation; or
- harassment, doxxing or attempts to contact a subject through this project.

## Corrections

Use `corrections/CORRECTION-TEMPLATE.md`. State the original claim, the supported correction, the source and the affected files. Preserve a transparent commit history.
