# Present-Market Use

## Positioning

IBLoud IVXX Story Lab is an independent transmedia research prototype. Its market value is not access to a celebrity. Its value is a reusable method for converting distributed creative practice into an auditable story, soundtrack-development process and rights record.

## Audiences and utility

| Audience | Useful artifact | Value offered |
|---|---|---|
| Artist management | Narrative map and boundaries | Understands organic audience meaning without adopting unsupported claims |
| Tour/venue marketing | Local, independent story activation | Adds contextual discovery while preserving the official sales channel |
| Music supervisors | Cue map and clearance ledger | Separates reference tracks from licensable candidates |
| Producers/songwriters | Character brief and human drafts | Supplies a defined world without requesting artist imitation |
| Researchers/educators | Source register and truth labels | Supports study of platform memory, celebrity and authorship |
| Funders/institutions | Method and contribution model | Makes process, public benefit and creator attribution inspectable |

## 2026 activation

The current market test centers on the September 18, 2026 Endless Summer Tour Part II stop at Mystic Lake Amphitheater in Shakopee, Minnesota.

### Funnel

1. **Discovery:** a concise Bluesky post introduces the archive.
2. **Context:** readers enter this repository or its Pages view.
3. **Trust:** the reader sees sources, non-affiliation language and rights status.
4. **Listen:** the reader may visit the Audio.com profile for research references.
5. **Action:** interested concertgoers follow the official Live Nation/Ticketmaster link.
6. **Learning:** Loptr Lab records aggregate, privacy-respecting outbound clicks.

### Measurement rules

Report only what the project can observe:

- Bluesky post views and engagement;
- repository visits;
- Audio.com plays reported by that platform;
- outbound clicks to an official ticket page; and
- documented inbound inquiries.

Do not call clicks “ticket sales.” Sales influence requires attributable conversion reporting from the ticketing or promotional partner.

## Recommended Bluesky copy

> Endless Summer returns Sept. 18 near Minneapolis. IBLoud IVXX began as a fragmented music-and-platform story; Loptr Lab is documenting what the 2026 return changes. Independent project—not affiliated with the tour. Research: [repository] Official tickets: [official link]

## Partnership threshold

The project becomes a tour marketing partner only after a written agreement defines:

- authorized names, marks and artwork;
- approved copy and territories;
- music and content clearances;
- compensation or referral attribution;
- data access and measurement;
- approval and takedown procedures; and
- whether any party receives ownership, license, commission or recoupment rights.
