# The Signal Between Us — Opening Screenplay Sample

**Development draft — September 10, 2026**  
**Approximate length:** 7 screenplay pages  
**Truth status:** FICTIONALIZED

> This sample contains original fictional scenes. The Rapper is not G-Eazy, Logic or another identifiable artist. No dialogue, conduct, private communication or participation is attributed to a real person. Public-interface imagery should be recreated for production unless separately cleared.

---

**FADE IN:**

## 1. BLACK SCREEN

A PHONE VIBRATES once.

Not a ringtone. A notification trying not to be noticed.

A green cursor blinks in the dark.

> NO RESULT FOUND.

The cursor waits.

A second line types itself.

> THAT DOESN'T MEAN IT WASN'T THERE.

## 2. INT. IBLoud'S APARTMENT — NIGHT

Minneapolis after midnight. Snow hangs beyond the glass without falling.

The room is lit by three things: an old mixing board, an open laptop, and the acid-green pulse of a router.

IBLOUD IVXX, thirties, headphones around her neck, moves between windows on her screen.

Not code.

Archives.

A dead link. A cached title with no body. A screenshot cropped too tightly to prove where it came from. A waveform named FINAL-final-2.

She drops the waveform onto a timeline.

A kick drum begins, then stops before it resolves.

On-screen folders:

> DOCUMENTED  
> ATTRIBUTED  
> INTERPRETIVE  
> FICTIONALIZED  
> UNRESOLVED

IBLoud drags the dead link into **UNRESOLVED**.

She opens a text file.

> THE HACKER AND THE RAPPER

She stares at it.

Deletes **THE**.

Now:

> HACKER / RAPPER

Deletes the slash.

Now nothing.

Her phone vibrates again.

A public notification: a concert announcement. The Rapper's silhouette above a date and a city.

IBLoud looks from the poster to the dead link.

She presses RECORD on the mixing board.

IBLOUD
(into the mic)
I remember the pitch.

The waveform draws her voice in cyan.

IBLOUD (CONT'D)
I don't remember the words.

She stops recording.

The silence that follows has tempo.

## 3. INT. HOTEL ELEVATOR — NIGHT

A different city. Mirrored walls. No windows.

THE RAPPER, thirties, dressed down after a performance, rides alone. His face is never shown cleanly—only fragments in reflection. Not concealment. Multiplication.

His phone fills with versions of himself.

A clip without its beginning.

A headline posed as a question.

A fan edit set to someone else's confession.

A post reads:

> HE ALWAYS DOES WHAT HE WANTS.

Another:

> ALL HE DOES IS SHINE.

The elevator numbers descend.

He locks the screen.

It lights again with another notification.

He powers the phone off.

For the first time all night, there is only one of him in the mirror.

The elevator opens.

A camera flash erupts from somewhere beyond the doors.

The mirror fractures him again.

## 4. INT. IBLoud'S APARTMENT — LATER

IBLoud builds a map on the wall.

Printed screenshots connected by removable green thread. Dates. URLs. Question marks.

At the center: an empty rectangle.

> MISSING PITCH — SOURCE UNKNOWN

She photographs the wall, then immediately photographs the photograph's metadata.

A ritual.

She notices something on one printout: the same phrase repeated beneath two unrelated posts.

> YOU HAD TO BE THERE.

She searches it.

Thousands of results.

She narrows by date.

Hundreds.

By platform.

Dozens.

By exact punctuation.

Two.

One is her own old post.

The other has been deleted.

IBLoud leans closer.

The deleted result retains a thumbnail: a dark stage, a person at a laptop, a person holding a microphone.

Too small to identify. Large enough to remember.

IBLOUD
There you are.

The router pulse quickens.

Or maybe she only notices it now.

She clicks.

> THIS CONTENT IS NO LONGER AVAILABLE.

IBLOUD
I can still see you.

She hears what she said.

Steps back.

IBLOUD (CONT'D)
No.

She moves the thumbnail from **DOCUMENTED** to **UNRESOLVED**.

Under it she writes:

> MEMORY IS NOT A RECEIPT.

## 5. INT. RECORDING STUDIO — NIGHT

The Rapper listens to a new beat with his PRODUCER. We hear only an original low-frequency pulse—no recognizable song or vocal style.

The Rapper lifts one ear of his headphones.

RAPPER
Kill the sample.

PRODUCER
It's cleared.

RAPPER
That's not what I said.

The Producer stops playback.

RAPPER (CONT'D)
I know what it belonged to before it belonged to us.

PRODUCER
That's every sample.

RAPPER
Exactly.

The Producer studies him.

PRODUCER
This about the archive girl?

The Rapper gives him a look.

PRODUCER (CONT'D)
Public account. Public repo. Public everything.

RAPPER
Public isn't the same as invited.

PRODUCER
She says that too.

The Rapper looks at the dark studio glass. Another mirror.

RAPPER
Then why am I a character?

## 6. INT. IBLoud'S APARTMENT — PRE-DAWN

IBLoud's screen displays the first paragraph of a story.

> A HACKER BREAKS INTO—

She deletes it.

Types:

> A HACKER ENTERS THE PUBLIC MACHINE BUILT AROUND A RAPPER.

She considers **HACKER**.

Opens a terminal window. Then closes it.

Opens an archive export.

IBLOUD
(to herself)
People hear hacker and think stolen secrets.

She types:

> SHE STEALS NOTHING.

Then:

> SHE STILL TAKES SOMETHING.

Her cursor stops.

The city outside begins turning from black to blue.

She opens a new document titled **CORRECTION PATH**.

## 7. INT. TOUR BUS — DAWN

The Rapper sits alone at a table as the bus moves through a nameless edge of city.

His phone is back on.

He reads IBLoud's public repository.

On-screen:

> THE RAPPER IS A FICTIONAL DRAMATIC ROLE UNLESS A NAMED PERSON EXPRESSLY AGREES TO PARTICIPATE.

He scrolls.

> ACCESS TO INFORMATION IS NOT PERMISSION.

He stops.

Scrolls back.

A tour manager, MARA, forties, precise without being cold, takes the seat across from him.

MARA
If it's false, counsel can send a letter.

RAPPER
What if it isn't false?

MARA
Which part?

RAPPER
The system.

MARA
Systems don't sign releases.

RAPPER
People build them.

MARA
People also hide inside that sentence.

He turns the phone toward her.

RAPPER
She left a door open.

MARA reads the page.

MARA
No. She labeled a door.

RAPPER
Difference?

MARA
A label tells you what she thinks it is. A contract tells you where it goes.

She hands the phone back.

MARA (CONT'D)
Do you want it gone?

He looks through the glass at a billboard bearing a face that resembles him only because the campaign has taught the city how to recognize it.

RAPPER
I want to know what happens if I answer.

## 8. INT. IBLoud'S APARTMENT — MORNING

IBLoud has fallen asleep at the desk.

The unfinished beat loops softly: signal, silence, signal, silence.

A GitHub notification appears.

> NEW ISSUE: PROJECT EVALUATION INQUIRY

She wakes.

Does not click immediately.

Instead, she opens a notebook. Writes the time. Takes a screenshot. Saves it to **DOCUMENTED**.

Only then does she open the message.

We do not see its author.

We see four words:

> WHO OWNS THE QUESTION?

IBLoud reads them twice.

She types a reply.

Stops.

Deletes it.

She creates a new file:

> TERMS-BEFORE-STORY.md

The beat reaches for resolution again.

This time, IBLoud lets it.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

> THE SIGNAL BETWEEN US

**END OF OPENING SAMPLE**

## Development notes

- The sample establishes the hacker's actual method as public-source archiving, not unauthorized access.
- The Rapper's identity remains fictional and visually uncast.
- No existing recording, lyric, logo, tour artwork, user interface or private message is required to produce these scenes.
- The inquiry at the end is fictional; it does not imply that a named artist or representative contacted the project.
- Later drafts should preserve each character's agency: IBLoud is not rewarded for projection, and the Rapper is not required to validate her interpretation.
