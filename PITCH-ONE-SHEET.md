# The Signal Between Us — Pitch One-Sheet

**Format:** Five-part limited series or feature film  
**Genre:** Techno-noir music drama / transmedia mystery  
**Development status:** Independent fictional treatment and research proof of concept  
**Created by:** Dominique Devereaux / IBLoud IVXX Story Lab  
**Current version:** September 11, 2026

> **Independent project.** No named artist, label, manager, promoter, venue, platform or attorney is represented as participating in or endorsing this project.

## Logline

After her partner takes her code and presents it to an Artist as his own MVP, a Minneapolis systems-builder discovers that she and the Artist are unknowingly fighting for the same creative freedom. To recover control of their work from the people and platforms that profit by keeping them apart, the hacker and the artist must decide whether collaboration is possible without turning either of them into content.

## The story

IBLoud IVXX does not break into private systems. She works inside what everyone can already see: deleted posts, headlines, recommendation loops, music fragments and the memories audiences carry between platforms.

The documented 2024 Tumblr story becomes her point of entry. Her partner has taken her code to the Artist as his own MVP. She responds by sharing an analysis for the Artist's investor and presenting herself as a bridge between creative and technical systems. The fictionalized Artist still experiences the wider archive as a threat: another person arranging his public life into a narrative he did not authorize. Both recognize part of the danger, but not yet the same antagonist.

Across five movements—**Signal, Noise, Feedback, Terms and Return**—they trace how the network rewards speculation, collapses context and makes recognition feel like relationship. IBLoud must surrender the fantasy that access creates entitlement. The rapper must decide whether protecting his agency requires silence, or whether a carefully bounded collaboration could create something neither of them could make alone.

The pitch survives in fragments. The ethical method for recovering authorship and negotiating participation becomes the story.

## Why this project

*The Signal Between Us* dramatizes a contemporary problem without treating a living person's private life as raw material: audiences are asked to feel intimacy through public signals, while creators lose control of the narratives built around them.

The project combines:

- a character-driven techno-noir story;
- music as emotional navigation rather than factual proof;
- visible truth-status labels for documentary, attributed, interpretive and fictional material;
- a correction path and auditable creative record; and
- a rights-aware model for participation, AI assistance and creator compensation.

Its value is not celebrity access. Its value is a repeatable way to turn fragmented public culture into accountable fiction.

## Principal characters

**IBLoud IVXX — the hacker / witness-artist**  
A Minneapolis systems researcher and musician who preserves what platforms discard. She wants to make the pattern legible. She must learn that an archive can harm the people it claims to preserve.

**The Rapper — a fictional dramatic role**  
A performer whose public identity has become shared property in the imagination of the network. He wants the freedom to act without every gesture becoming evidence. He must decide whether participation can be negotiated without surrendering control.

**The Network — the antagonist**  
Feeds, headlines, recommendation systems and audience certainty operating as one pressure system. It does not need to lie; it only needs to remove context faster than people can restore it.

## Format and episode movement

| Part | Movement | Dramatic question |
|---|---|---|
| I | Signal | Who sent the story into motion? |
| II | Noise | When does recognition become projection? |
| III | Feedback | Can an archive become a weapon without intending to? |
| IV | Terms | What would ethical participation actually require? |
| V | Return | Can attention be redirected without manufacturing affiliation? |

The same architecture can be developed as a contained feature, with the five movements functioning as acts.

## Audience and tone

For audiences drawn to music-world drama, digital mystery, creator culture and morally complicated partnerships. The tone is nocturnal, intimate and technically curious: contaminated black, acid green and cyan; fractured interfaces; quiet rooms beneath loud public spectacle.

The work should feel emotionally immediate without presenting invented scenes as biography or reenactment.
