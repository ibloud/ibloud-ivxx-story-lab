# Rights and Clearance Framework

This document is an organizational checklist, not legal advice and not evidence that an asset is cleared.

## Rights layers

| Layer | Question | Evidence required before commercial release |
|---|---|---|
| Composition | Who wrote the melody and lyrics? | Split sheet, publisher data and licenses |
| Sound recording | Who owns the recorded performance? | Master ownership record or master-use license |
| Samples/stems | Does the file contain another recording? | Written sample/master and composition clearances |
| Performer | Who sang or played? | Performer release and compensation terms |
| Production | Who created the beat, session or arrangement? | Producer agreement and royalty/fee terms |
| Name/likeness | Does use imply identity or endorsement? | Written permission when required; avoid misleading presentation |
| Artwork/photography | Who owns the image? | License, assignment or original-creation record |
| Platform/AI | What contractual rights govern output? | Terms version, account tier, creation date and permitted download record |
| Distribution | Can the asset be monetized and delivered? | Aggregator terms, metadata, identifiers and territorial scope |

## Mashup rule

Applying a Creative Commons license to a mashup does not grant permission over third-party compositions or sound recordings. Until every required owner authorizes the use, mashups are research references and are not distributed through this repository.

## Proof-of-concept excerpt rule

The proof-of-concept may propose brief excerpts from publicly available material only when each excerpt performs a specific critical or analytical function. The editor must log the source, exact in/out points, duration, claim supported, transformative purpose, amount used, audio treatment and alternatives considered.

The prototype must not use a complete song, a music-video sequence for atmosphere alone, an excerpt longer than the analytical purpose requires, or editing that suggests participation or endorsement. Source audio is muted by default; retain only the minimum words or sound necessary to analyze the source itself. The persistent notice is:

> UNOFFICIAL NONCOMMERCIAL RESEARCH PROOF OF CONCEPT — LIMITED EXCERPTS USED FOR COMMENTARY AND ANALYSIS — NOT AFFILIATED WITH OR ENDORSED BY G-EAZY OR THE ENDLESS SUMMER TOUR — RIGHTS AND FAIR-USE REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE RELEASE

See `FAIR-USE-PROOF-OF-CONCEPT.md`. Fair use is a fact-specific legal doctrine, not a clearance status or permission created by a label.

## Suno rule

Suno may be used to make development demos only when:

- inputs are original or authorized;
- the account tier permits the intended use;
- the output is downloaded through a permitted channel;
- the prompt does not request a living artist's identity or recognizable voice;
- the human author preserves lyrics and substantive revisions; and
- AI-generated material is accurately disclosed.

Contractual permission to use an output is distinct from U.S. copyrightability. Human-written lyrics, performances, selection, arrangement and substantive modifications may have different protection from material generated solely by AI.
