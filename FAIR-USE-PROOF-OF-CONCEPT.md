# Fair-Use Proof-of-Concept Protocol

## Intended purpose

The trailer is an unofficial, noncommercial research prototype demonstrating the narrative and documentation method described in this repository. Its purpose is to criticize and analyze how public media fragments become platform memory, character material, cultural interpretation and present-market attention. It is not a substitute music video, concert film, biography, advertisement or compilation of G-Eazy footage.

This protocol records a good-faith rationale; it is not legal advice and does not guarantee that a court or platform will find a particular use fair.

## Required labels

### Opening card — minimum 3 seconds

> UNOFFICIAL NONCOMMERCIAL RESEARCH PROOF OF CONCEPT  
> Limited excerpts proposed for criticism, commentary and analysis.  
> Not affiliated with or endorsed by G-Eazy or the Endless Summer Tour.

### Persistent identifier

Display `UNOFFICIAL RESEARCH POC` in the safe-title area whenever a third-party excerpt appears.

### Closing card — minimum 5 seconds

> Independent Loptr Lab / IBLoud IVXX research prototype.  
> Public facts, interpretation and fiction are separately labeled.  
> Rights and fair-use review required before public or commercial release.  
> Sources and corrections: repository URL

### Metadata description

> Unofficial, noncommercial research proof of concept demonstrating the IBLoud IVXX Story Lab method. Limited third-party excerpts, if present, are included only where necessary for source identification, criticism, commentary and analysis. No affiliation, endorsement or private knowledge is claimed. Rights and fair-use review remains required.

## Four-factor working analysis

| Factor | Prototype practice | Risk control |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose and character | Research, criticism, commentary and transformation | Narration or text must analyze every excerpt; no decorative montage |
| Nature of source | Publicly released audiovisual works may be highly creative | Prefer factual interviews, public statements and documentary material when they prove the point |
| Amount and substantiality | Only what is necessary to identify and analyze the source | Default target: 1–4 seconds; justify anything longer; avoid the expressive heart of a song or video |
| Market effect | Prototype should not replace demand for the originals | No complete performances, songs, hooks or coherent substitute viewing experience; link to official sources |

All four factors must be considered together. There is no automatic safe number of seconds.

## Clip admission test

An excerpt enters the working cut only if every answer below is recorded:

1. What exact claim or research question does the excerpt support?
2. Why would a still, citation, paraphrase or original recreation be insufficient?
3. What is the shortest usable in/out range?
4. What narration, juxtaposition or annotation transforms its purpose?
5. Is original audio necessary? If not, mute it.
6. Could the edit imply endorsement, private knowledge or official tour participation?
7. Does the excerpt disclose more than necessary about a living person?
8. Is the source URL, publication date and access date logged?

If the answers are incomplete, use a placeholder rather than the excerpt.

## Editorial constraints

- Do not download, circumvent access controls or remove watermarks from platform media.
- Use locally supplied, lawfully obtained source files or authorized review copies.
- Do not synchronize G-Eazy recordings, mashups or other commercial music as trailer score.
- Do not imitate the voice or visual likeness of a living artist with generative media.
- Clearly label reenactments, generated connective imagery and fictionalized material.
- Do not use tour, label, NFL or artist logos as branding.
- Preserve original context; do not manufacture statements through deceptive cutting.
- Maintain a correction and takedown contact route.

## Review gates

| Gate | Required result |
|---|---|
| Internal rough cut | Every third-party frame appears in `TRAILER-CLIP-LEDGER.csv` |
| Private pitch screening | Opening, persistent and closing labels are visible and readable |
| Public upload | Entertainment-law review of the final locked cut and platform terms |
| Monetization, sponsorship or paid media | Written licenses/approvals or a new legal analysis reflecting commercial purpose |
| Broadcaster or CTV delivery | Errors-and-omissions review, cue sheet, releases, delivery specs and network approval |

## Status vocabulary

- `PLACEHOLDER` — source identified; no media placed in the repository.
- `RATIONALE DRAFTED` — proposed use and four-factor reasoning recorded.
- `COUNSEL REVIEWED` — final locked use reviewed by qualified counsel; not the same as licensed.
- `LICENSED` — written authorization covers the actual use.
- `REMOVED` — excerpt rejected or replaced.

Never use `FAIR USE CLEARED` as a status.
